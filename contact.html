Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding shipping, warranty coverage, and custom build turnaround times.

1. How are prebuilt systems and fragile components packaged for shipping?

All graphics cards are secured internally with custom Instapak expanding high-density foam brackets. The entire chassis is double-boxed with impact-absorbing corners and insured for 100% of its full replacement value with tracked courier delivery.

2. What is the turnaround time for a custom commissioned build?

Upon component validation, our assembly, custom cable combing, and 24-hour thermal stress-testing workflow takes between 24 to 48 hours before immediate dispatch.

3. Can I customize or swap individual parts on pre-configured rigs?

Absolutely. You can use our interactive "Custom PC Builder" on the homepage or chat directly with our engineering team on Telegram to tailor any component to your exact preferences.
